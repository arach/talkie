background material; the Talkie UI, icon, and typography are composed from
project sources by `compose.swift` and `compose-ipad.swift`.

## Mineral instrument panel — message-first machine

Edit target: `mineral-instrument-panel-v3.png`. This pass rebuilds the physical
object around the campaign hierarchy: one reserved typography fascia, one
precisely aligned display bay, and narrow control rails.

```text

Preserve the same premium photorealistic materials, restrained pale mineral blue-gray color, warm copper indicator accents, centered orthographic camera, and soft studio lighting. Keep exact 4:3 landscape composition.

Rebuild the geometry around these exact needs:

- Create a clean, quiet horizontal information fascia across the top, aligned with the central display: approximately x 12% to 88% and y 4% to 16% of the canvas. It must be a straight-on rounded rectangle with uniform thin bezel, exact symmetrical alignment, no perspective skew, no inner artwork, and a flat blank mineral surface. The thin bezel should feel modern and take very little space.
- Push all physical controls entirely into narrow left and right side rails outside the display bay. Use fewer, more compact controls: one medium dial, a short fader, and three tiny copper LEDs on the left; two small dials, one medium copper-ring dial, and a compact speaker grille on the right.

- Make the machine feel slimmer, cleaner, more architectural, and more contemporary than the reference while retaining a subtle 1970s hi-fi / retrofuturist character.
- Maintain continuous material and lighting from top to bottom. No white gradient, no pale header wash, no abrupt texture change, no dark black surround, no dramatic contrast.
- No letters, numbers, words, UI, iPad, phone, screen content, logos, badges, decorative markings, or watermark anywhere.

Critical geometry: the large central bay must visually match a later overlay that begins at 12.35% from the left, 16.3% from the top, is 75.3% of the canvas wide, and ends at 92.2% from the top. The top fascia is reserved solely for typography and must read as intentional product architecture.
```
