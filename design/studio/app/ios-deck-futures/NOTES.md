# iOS Deck Futures

**Status:** Study
**Surface:** `/ios-deck-futures`
**Implementation baseline:** `codex/talkie-voice-deck`

## Purpose

Bring three repo-grounded external reviews into one durable Studio surface:

- **Kimi** — ten ranked improvements to the existing iPhone command deck.
- **Grok** — five product ideas that carry voice into context, agents, and action.
- **Opus** — five genuinely different iPad command-deck compositions.

The page is a review board, not a committed roadmap. It inherits the current
Porcelain blue/off-white direction and keeps the already-approved Detent Hub
study intact.

## Strongest convergence

1. Establish Porcelain as the reliable deck identity.
2. Give the live console a strict glance model.
3. Put delivery mode in the keybed and make lane changes reachable.
4. Consolidate Details, History, and Readouts around one task context.
5. Prototype **The Bridge** first on iPad, with **Flight Recorder** as the
   strongest contrasting direction.

## Scope boundary

This study does not replace `/ios-deck-detent-hub`, change Swift, or assert that
all twenty concepts should ship. It makes the alternatives inspectable and
comparable before implementation.
