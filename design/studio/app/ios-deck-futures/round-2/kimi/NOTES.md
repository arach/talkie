# Round 2 · Kimi — The Tethered Table

**Status:** At-bat

## Spatial premise

One task lies open on the table as a full sheet of paper; the other tasks
wait as a fanned deck of slips at the left thumb; Talkie's voice disc sits
at the right thumb, tied to the open task by a thread — voice travels along
the thread, and the thread is the targeting.

## Interaction notes

- Tap a slip in the fan to bring that task to the table (the current sheet
  returns to the fan). No sidebar; selection is direct manipulation.
- Hold the disc to talk. The thread carries a current while listening;
  release and a droplet rides the thread into the sheet's tie ring.
- Mac unreachable: the bridge owns the header, the thread sags and cools,
  the disc keeps recording locally ("kept on this iPad · delivers on
  reconnect"), and one Reconnect path leads. Endpoint evidence stays behind
  Connection details.
- Canvas is a fixed 1180×885 (12.9-inch iPad landscape, 4:3) so the thread
  geometry is authored, not derived.

## Honesty

Illustrative task state, shared with the Round 1 at-bats. No progress
percentages, token counts, queue depth, or telemetry the bridge does not
provide. "Needs you" remains answerable only in Codex Desktop on Studio Mac.
