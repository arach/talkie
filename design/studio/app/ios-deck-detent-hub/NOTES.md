# Detent Hub

Refinement study for Grok's Direction A. This is a Studio-only comparison;
it does not change the Swift deck.

## Held constant

- Positions 01 and 04 use the same rectangular keytop, edge, depth, and press
  response as the rest of the deck.
- The specialized control is a 60pt circular instrument seated inside that
  shared keytop, not a replacement for it.
- Phone, Watch, and Silent are discrete physical detents, not a continuous value.
- Silent sits left, Watch sits at the crown, and Phone sits right. No output
  detent or pointer approaches from the bottom.
- One moving indicator connects the hub to the selected detent.
- Position 04 is a paired dark LED status window inside the same circular housing.
- Scope light uses brass amber for active state and red only for failure. No green.
- The complete key remains at least a 44pt touch target.

## Permutations

### A1 · Needle Ports

The closest refinement of Grok A. The center hub is quieter, the needle is
longer, and each peripheral port is large enough to survive the true-size
render. Selection reads as a physical contact: needle meets lit port.

### A2 · Detent Carriage

The needle terminates in a captive carriage that cups the selected port. It
spends more visual weight on the active destination and less on the center
knob. The paired status lens separates the code from the motion trace.

## Evaluation rubric

1. Selected output is legible at 60pt without reading a text label.
2. The three available stops remain apparent in every state.
3. MIC and PLAY motion are distinguishable from RDY and RUN.
4. The pair feels like one instrument family beside Mapper and Spaces.
5. Geometry is feasible in SwiftUI without bitmap assets.

## Current recommendation

Start from A2's carriage selector and A1's simpler LED capsule, both seated in
the shared rectangular keytop. That synthesis has the clearest active
destination, the quietest status face, and the strongest connection to the
rest of the deck.
