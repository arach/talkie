# Detent Hub

Second refinement study for Grok's Direction A. This is a Studio-only
comparison; it does not change the Swift deck.

## Held constant

- Positions 01 and 04 use the same rectangular keytop, edge, depth, and press
  response as the rest of the deck.
- The specialized control is a 64pt circular instrument seated inside that
  shared keytop, not a replacement for it.
- Phone, Watch, and Silent are discrete physical detents, not a continuous value.
- Silent sits left, Watch sits at the crown, and Phone sits right. No output
  detent or pointer approaches from the bottom.
- One moving indicator connects the hub to the selected detent.
- Position 04 is a paired dark LED status window inside the same circular housing.
- Porcelain light uses cobalt for active state and red only for failure. No green.
- The complete key remains at least a 44pt touch target.

## Permutations

### B1 · Twin Core

The active route moves into the smoked-glass center at a useful size while the
three crown stops remain visible. Position 04 uses the same core diameter and
material, turning two special controls into one instrument family.

### B2 · Crown Rail

The crown opens into a stronger selector rail and the status eye grows slightly
to give the trace more room. It is the expressive alternate, not the default.

## Evaluation rubric

1. Selected output is legible at 64pt without reading a text label.
2. The three available stops remain apparent in every state.
3. MIC and PLAY motion are distinguishable from RDY and RUN.
4. The pair feels like one instrument family beside Mapper and Spaces.
5. Geometry is feasible in SwiftUI without bitmap assets.

## Current recommendation

Start from B1 Twin Core. Its large center route identity distinguishes Watch
from iPhone at true size, the matched status eye feels deliberate, and the
porcelain seat connects both controls to the rest of the deck. B2 is useful if
the selector needs more character after physical-device testing.
